Delete your MiniPort account

This page is the public account-deletion resource for MiniPort.

Fastest option

If you are signed in to MiniPort, open Account → Delete account and confirm the deletion. The production app must complete the deletion of the account and associated personal data, except data that MiniPort is legally required to retain.

If you no longer have the app

Send a deletion request from the account's registered contact to support@miniport.app. Include your registered mobile number and state that you want the MiniPort account deleted. We may use a verification step to protect the account from unauthorised deletion.

Operator action required before store submission: replace this placeholder contact with the real MiniPort privacy/support inbox and ensure the request is actually processed within the published retention/deletion period.

What is deleted

Account profile information, saved addresses, GST details, notification identifiers, KYC/driver information and other personal data associated with the account should be deleted when the request is completed. Records that must be retained for accounting, fraud prevention, disputes or legal obligations may be retained only to the extent required and should be separated/anonymised where legally appropriate.
