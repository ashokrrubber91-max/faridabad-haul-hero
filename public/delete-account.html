Delete your MiniPort account

This page is the public account-deletion resource for MiniPort. MiniPort is a local goods-transport booking service.

Delete inside the app (fastest)

Sign in to MiniPort, open Account, choose Delete account, and confirm. The deletion runs immediately on MiniPort's servers, signs the account out everywhere, and removes the login itself. An account cannot be deleted while a trip is still open — finish or cancel the trip first.

If you no longer have the app

Send a deletion request from the account's registered mobile number to the MiniPort privacy contact published on the current store listing, stating that you want the MiniPort account deleted. A verification step is used so that nobody else can delete an account.

Operator configuration required before store submission: publish the real MiniPort privacy/support inbox on this page and in the store listing, and confirm requests are processed within the published deletion period. No support address is claimed here because it is not part of the application source.

What is deleted

Name, mobile number, email/login, saved addresses, GST/business details, payout and bank details, notification identifiers, driver identity and vehicle documents (including the stored files), driver application and verification records, and any unused reward balance.

What is kept, and why

Completed trip, invoice, payment and payout records are retained without personal identifiers, because they are required for tax, accounting, fraud-prevention and dispute purposes, and because the other party to a trip has a legitimate record of it. Retained records are detached from the deleted account and cannot be used to sign in or to identify the person.

Timing

In-app deletion is immediate. Requests sent by message are completed after identity verification.
