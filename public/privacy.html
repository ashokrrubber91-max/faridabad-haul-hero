MiniPort Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 September 2026

MiniPort is a local goods-transport booking service. This policy explains the information MiniPort collects, why it is used, when it is shared, and how users can request deletion.

Information we collect

  • Account information: name, mobile number, authentication identifiers and account role.
  • Trip information: pickup/drop addresses, coordinates, stops, sender/receiver contact details, vehicle choice, booking status, payment method and trip notes.
  • Location: precise device location when a user explicitly uses location features; verified drivers may share live location during an active service flow.
  • Driver verification: KYC/identity documents, driving/vehicle information, vehicle number, vehicle photos, insurance and payout information when a user applies to drive.
  • Payments: payment status and transaction identifiers. Card/UPI credentials are processed by the applicable payment provider and are not stored by MiniPort as raw payment credentials.
  • Photos/files: KYC documents and proof-of-delivery images where the relevant feature is used.
  • Notifications/support: device notification tokens, support messages and service communications.

How we use information

We use information to create and manage bookings, match customers and drivers, calculate fares, provide navigation and live tracking, verify drivers, process payments, prevent fraud and abuse, provide customer support, send service notifications, issue invoices and meet legal/accounting obligations.

Third-party services

Depending on the feature and production configuration, MiniPort may use Supabase for authentication/database/storage, Google Maps for maps/geocoding/routing, Firebase for push notifications, Razorpay for payments, SMS providers for OTP/SMS delivery, and an AI/support provider for support features. Their processing is subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

Location

Location is used only for relevant booking, navigation and driver-tracking functionality. If a user declines location permission, the app provides manual address entry where the feature allows it. Driver live location is access-controlled and is used for an active trip/service purpose.

Sharing

Trip information is shared with the customer, assigned driver and authorised MiniPort operations personnel only as needed to provide the service. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and deletion

We retain information only as long as needed for service delivery, security, fraud prevention, accounting, dispute handling and legal obligations. Users can initiate account deletion from the MiniPort account settings. Deletion removes associated personal data that we are not legally required to retain. Any legally retained records are limited to the applicable obligation and are protected from normal account use.

Security

MiniPort uses authenticated access controls, database row-level security, server-side financial controls and protected storage/access patterns. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Privacy contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the privacy/support contact published with the current MiniPort store listing and website. The contact details must remain current before a store submission.

Updates

We may update this policy when the service or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top will be updated when material changes are made.
