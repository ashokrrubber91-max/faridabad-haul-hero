MiniPort Terms of Service

Effective date: 14 September 2026

Service

MiniPort connects customers requesting local goods transport with verified transport partners where available. Availability, ETA and pricing may depend on live traffic, vehicle availability and the selected service.

Customer responsibilities

  • Provide accurate pickup/drop details and contact information.
  • Declare goods truthfully and do not request prohibited, illegal, hazardous or unsafe transport.
  • Ensure the sender/receiver is available for required pickup/drop verification.
  • Pay the final amount shown by the applicable booking/payment flow.

Driver responsibilities

  • Maintain valid licence, registration, insurance and required KYC.
  • Use only the approved vehicle configuration and comply with applicable transport laws.
  • Protect customer goods and use the required pickup/drop verification process.

Fares, waiting and payments

Fare configuration is controlled by MiniPort's server-side vehicle catalogue. Loading/unloading waiting charges apply only according to the configured free allowance and server-recorded stage timestamps. A failed online payment is not itself a ride cancellation; where supported, the same booking can be switched to an allowed alternative payment method.

Cancellations and refunds

Cancellation reasons are recorded against the booking. Refund timing depends on the payment provider and the applicable transaction state. Nothing in these terms removes rights that cannot legally be excluded.

Safety

Use the in-trip Emergency 112 action where immediate emergency assistance is required. Trip sharing is provided for safety and convenience; shared links may contain trip information, so users should share them only with people they trust.

Prohibited goods

Explosives, ammunition, firearms, flammable liquids/gases, illegal/contraband goods, narcotics, hazardous/toxic chemicals, live animals/birds, human remains/body parts and other goods prohibited by law or MiniPort policy are not accepted.

Contact and policy documents

Current privacy, deletion and support contact details must be kept current on the public MiniPort website/store listing before production release.
